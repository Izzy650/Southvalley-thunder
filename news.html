Thunder Clinch Top Spot in Central Valley League With Dominant 3–1 Victory

Southvalley Thunder delivered a commanding performance at Bakersfield Sports Village, dismantling Valley Kings FC 3–1 in what was arguably the team's finest display of the 2026 season. Luis Herrera opened the scoring in the 12th minute with a clinical finish, Marco Silva doubled the lead before halftime with a stunning long-range effort, and Elijah Washington sealed the result in the 71st minute. The win puts Thunder seven points clear at the top of the Central Valley League with just four games remaining.

"This team has been incredible all season," said head coach Rafael Dominguez. "The way they press, the way they move the ball — every single player is committed to the system. We're not done yet."
