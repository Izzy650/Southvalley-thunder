U-16 Boys Clinch First Place With Dominant 3–1 Win Over Valley Kings

Southvalley Thunder's U-16 Boys delivered a commanding performance at Bakersfield Sports Village, defeating Valley Kings FC 3–1 in one of their best displays of the season. The win puts them seven points clear at the top of the Central Valley League table with four games to play. Head coach Brian Thompson credited the team's pressing game and clinical finishing.
