Our Story

About Thunder

A Bakersfield soccer club built on development, community, and a love for the game.

Our Mission

Developing Players. Building Character.

Southvalley Thunder was founded in 2018 with a simple belief: every kid in Bakersfield deserves access to quality soccer training and a positive team environment.

We offer competitive and recreational programs for players ages U-8 through U-19. Our certified coaching staff focuses on technical skill development, tactical understanding, and above all, a love for the game.

Whether your child is just getting started or working toward a college or professional career, Thunder is a place where players grow at every level.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

⚡
Excellence

We hold our players and coaches to the highest standards — not just in results, but in attitude, effort, and sportsmanship every single day.

🤝
Respect

Respect for teammates, opponents, coaches, and the game itself is non-negotiable at Southvalley Thunder.

💪
Resilience

We teach players how to handle adversity, learn from failure, and bounce back stronger — skills that go far beyond the soccer field.

🌱
Development

Long-term player development over short-term results. We build complete players, not just winners of youth tournaments.

🏘️
Community

Thunder is more than a club — it's a family. We are deeply connected to Bakersfield and invest back into our community.

🎉
Fun

Soccer should be enjoyable. We make sure every player — from U-8 to U-19 — has fun, makes friends, and loves the game.

Our Journey

Club History

2018
Club Founded

Southvalley Thunder is established in Bakersfield with just two age groups — U-10 and U-12 — and a group of passionate coaches and parents.

2019
Expansion to U-8 through U-16

The club grows rapidly, adding six new age groups and surpassing 100 registered players in just our second season.

2021
First Regional Championship

Our U-14 Boys team wins the Central Valley Regional title — the club's first major championship.

2023
Back-to-Back Titles

U-16 Boys win the Central Valley League title, and we expand to full U-8 through U-19 programs for both boys and girls.

2025
New Training Facility

Thunder partners with Bakersfield Sports Village for a permanent home facility, giving all teams access to professional-quality training grounds.

2026
Today

280+ players across 12 age groups. Three championships. And just getting started.
